# Community participation

Keep investigation and engineering discussions respectful, specific and evidence based.
Disagree with ideas without harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing or personal
attacks. Do not publish private case data or another person's information.

Maintainers may moderate project discussions and contributions that violate these rules.
Report sensitive concerns through the private contact route described in `SECURITY.md`
when available. Do not publish private details merely to request moderation. No external
organization, support staff or guaranteed response process is implied.
