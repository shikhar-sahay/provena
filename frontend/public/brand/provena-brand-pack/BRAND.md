# Provena Brand Identity

## Core idea

The Provena mark combines a geometric **P** with connected provenance nodes. It represents evidence lineage, traceability, chain of custody, and relationships between investigative artifacts.

## Visual character

- Minimal, precise, technical, and restrained
- Monochrome-first identity
- Designed for both dark and light interfaces
- Avoid decorative cybersecurity clichés such as shields, padlocks, fingerprints, hacker imagery, and neon effects

## Primary usage

Use the symbol plus `PROVENA` wordmark for prominent brand surfaces such as the login screen, documentation, and presentation material. Use the symbol alone for compact navigation, favicons, and app icons.

## Color

The logo is intentionally monochrome.

- Dark UI: white mark and wordmark on near-black surfaces
- Light UI: near-black mark and wordmark on white or near-white surfaces
- Do not introduce gradients into the production mark
- Semantic application colors should not recolor the logo

## Clear space

Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least one node diameter. Do not crowd the logo with borders, text, or controls.

## Small sizes

At favicon sizes, use the symbol alone. Fine details should remain secondary to the recognizable P silhouette.
